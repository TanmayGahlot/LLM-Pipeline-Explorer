Pairwise Cosine Angle & Attention Logits

Understanding the core mathematics of vector search and Transformer self-attention.

1. COSINE_SIMILARITY
cos(θ) = (u • v) / (||u|| × ||v||)

Measures angular alignment regardless of vector length. Bounds: -1.0 to +1.0.

2. SCALED_DOT_PRODUCT (ATTENTION)
Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(QKT / √d)V

The raw unnormalized dot-product powering the Transformer self-attention mechanism.

3. EUCLIDEAN_DISTANCE (L2)
d(u, v) = √∑(ui - vi)²

Geometric straight-line distance, susceptible to the curse of dimensionality.
